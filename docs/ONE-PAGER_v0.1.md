## The problem, and why it is not a staffing problem

Every OPO is federally required to run a QAPI program (42 CFR 486.348). Every OPO staffs it as a **department** — and the department works *retrospectively, on a sample*: chart audits begin ~7 days after recovery and ship within 30 `[H]`; review covers the fraction human attention can reach; findings arrive weeks after the case they describe; corrective actions are tracked in spreadsheets and verified, when they are verified, by asking. Survey preparation is an event. The result is the quality profession's open secret, spoken at the site that seeded this design: with the right checks built into the record, most of the checking disappears — the department's size was never a measure of its people; **it is the record system's missing checks, made visible as headcount.** Which closes a loop this estate already built: an EDR without a floor *requires* a quality department to be its floor, after the fact. The #1 headache (the EDR) and the #2 headache (quality) are two costs of one missing object — **a record that verifies itself.** REGISTRAR ships the record. SURVEYOR is its verification machinery, given its own name and pointed at the whole operation, continuously.

## The reframe — quality is a property of the record, not a department

**Hazards unreachable by construction, never forbidden by policy.** The audit moves from the week after to the keystroke: a chart that is missing its ABO subtype, its second signature, its attached consent recording, or its inspection-timestamp ordering does not become a nonconformity three weeks later — *it does not close.* Three inversions, each a number the leadership above quality can read: **sample → census** (100% of records checked at ingest, not 5–10% at month-end) · **retrospective → present-tense** (the finding arrives while the case can still be fixed) · **event → property** (the survey binder is a fold over the tape, current every morning). And the half of quality that is *not* mechanical — investigation, root cause, effectiveness, culture — stops running on anecdote and gets instruments.

## The proof this is real, not aspiration `[H]`

The design's requirements document was dictated by a quality department itself, into a recorder, during its own cross-departmental teaching program. The harvest of that corpus (ten transcripts, ten miners, every line read) yielded: **(1)** a deduplicated catalog of **~40 deterministic checks** the department performs by hand — cross-document identity matching, two-person ABO/HLA signature gates, timestamp-independence tests, DRE branching completeness, controlled-vocabulary enforcement, revision control against the document system, and a full **SLA lattice** (≤7d audit start · ≤30d to processor · 24h transmission-report and reactive-serology clocks · 72h/12h/48h/1h draw windows · 1h15m prep-to-incision · 15h/24h refrigeration clocks · 5-business-day feedback measured from the *correct anchor field* — itself a historical error mode · 30/60-day federal submissions · 1.5h onsite SLA · 30-minute offer cadence); **(2)** the flagship gap: an allocation act that is a federally reportable violation has *zero system check today* and is recovered by phone calls — one organ, one primary offer, enforced by culture alone; **(3)** the demand signal for the non-mechanical half, in the quality manager's own words: an abandoned policy↔regulation crosswalk ("you just can't maintain that"), an explicit wish for machinery that reads regulations against policies ("there's no mechanism"), and the PHI constraint that dictates the architecture; **(4)** the design's own governing caution, spoken unprompted by a coordinator: *"if you ping me every time I chart something, I'm going to not chart a thing"* — Goodhart, live; **(5)** the department's stated doctrine — *variance is attributed to system and process, not the person* — which is Just Culture, professed and unenforceable. SURVEYOR is the version that can keep that promise.

## The architecture — three altitudes, one tape, and the crosswalk

- **THE FLOOR — deterministic, model-free, and it is the product even if everything else fails.** The harvested check catalog compiled as gates that run at data entry on every record. The temporal rules are a Simple Temporal Network: consistency ⟺ no negative cycle, computed in the (min,+) tropical semiring, integer minutes, exactly associative, bit-identical under acceleration — **the same floor REGISTRAR already ships**, re-aimed from one case's deadlines to the whole operation's. Zero AI. The floor must pass its full battery with every learned component disabled, and any resident must beat the floor or neither ships. `[D from H; unimplemented]`
- **THE RESIDENT — quality in the present tense.** A standing occupant of the stream (FUSOR at the heart) that judges, at each boundary, whether *this instant* deserves attention: the case drifting toward a missed window, the contradiction between pages, the silence where a lab should be. The alert-fatigue disease that killed every clinical alerting system is a measured impossibility for thresholds — the best fixed dial caught 36/39 and was deaf where it mattered; dial-zero fired 921/hr; judgment absorbed into weights ran 9.5× quieter with the catches kept `[M — inherited from dev streams; transfer is a BET]`. Every hold is on the tape with its margin: **silence becomes auditable.** Feedback lands on the case, never the coordinator's phone — the Goodhart law from the harvest, honored structurally.
- **THE FIELD — the instruments for the unmechanical half, gated.** Variance investigation gets the trilemma (was it coverage, novelty, or distortion — a mechanical system-vs-person discriminator, which *is* Just Culture as architecture). Effectiveness gets the second ledger (below). Handoffs get occlusion analysis; underreporting gets a measured lower bound; the unwritten rule followed 19-of-19 times gets found (positive deviance, mined not surveyed). Anything person-adjacent — calibration, per-seat readings — sits behind the consent gate: **the subject reads their own number first**, and there is no machine path from any reading to a verdict about a person. `[SPEC; PARALLAX line, its own pre-registration]`
- **THE CROSSWALK — the thing the quality manager asked for by name.** A frontier model reads the *public* material — OPTN policy editions, CMS rules, FDA and AATB standards, with byte-pinned citations — and maintains the diff against the site's own controlled documents as a standing fold: edition changes surfaced with lead time, affected policies named, nothing invented (a quote is admissible only if it byte-matches a pinned source). **Two models, two data classes, one clean line:** frontier reads public regulation — no PHI, no BAA; a local open-weight model reads the site — and never egresses. The split is not a compliance decoration; it is what makes the design procurable at a 40-person OPO. `[SPEC; REGISTRAR's cite.py machinery, re-aimed]`
- **THE SECOND LEDGER — the part leadership sees.** Every finding, hold, variance, CAPA, and expectation lands on an append-only, hash-chained tape. The monthly line-of-sight report, the QAPI committee packet, the governing-board December review, and the survey binder are **folds over that tape — generated, current, and citing their receipts.** The books close continuously. A surveyor's question becomes a query.

## CAPA is a patch, and PDSA was always the lifecycle

Every corrective action is a typed row: owner · falsifiable expectation with a resolve-by date · expiry (nothing is permanent by default) · inverse (a wrong fix unwinds, not scars). Effectiveness — "sustained corrective action," the phrase the department already uses `[H]` — stops being a claim and becomes an automatically graded expectation: the system re-checks, at the horizon, whether the variance class actually fell. **Plan-Do-Study-Act is the patch lifecycle wearing its quality-profession name:** Plan = draft with expectation · Do = shadow · Study = grade · Act = mount under signature or retire with the reason printed. The QAPI committee is the ratification surface; one yes mounts; drift demotes.

## What it never does

Never grades, ranks, or scores a person (it reads records, structures, and flows; the trilemma exists precisely to separate system from person) · never files a regulatory report or communicates with a surveyor, agency, or registry — surfacing is internal; reporting decisions are human, per the site's own escalation doctrine `[H: "we don't self-report without a conversation first"]` · never writes the quality record — findings enter under a human name · never auto-applies a CAPA — the seam is a signature · never sends PHI anywhere — local model, zero egress, air-gapped supported · never holds the org hostage — MIT, full export, no license server, no vendor in the loop. **There is no pricing page, no contact form, and no ask anywhere in the repository.**

## The deciders — pre-registered, cheapest first

- **F-RETRO — the flagship, and it is a work order now, not a design.** Implement the floor catalog; run it against N quarters of the site's *historical closed charts*; score against the quality department's own findings log. Report **recall** (fraction of their historical findings reproduced, at ingest-time instead of week-three), **precision**, **the surplus** (real findings from the census the sample never touched), and **audit-hours per case, before/after**. Backtestable, zero operational risk, PHI never leaves the building. `[NULL]` #1: the existing sampled human audit. `[NULL]` #2: a dumb checklist script on a cron — the floor must beat the checklist's naive form or the sophistication is decoration.
- **F-CROSSWALK:** one real edition change (the AATB 15th→16th transition the harvest recorded is the natural fixture `[H]`) — the frontier arm produces the policy-impact diff; graded against the quality staff's own manual reading. Dies if it misses material changes or floods immaterial ones.
- **F-FIXTURE:** the synthetic OPO world with planted nonconformities — the F-PATCH-DELTA machinery reused; doubles as the PARALLAX plant battery. A pass bounds from above; the real-site arm is F-RETRO.
- **R1-gate:** a quality director reads the floor catalog and **recognizes their own audit checklist.** If they don't, the spine is wrong and nothing above it matters.
- Standing honesty: the resident tier inherits the vise-transfer `[BET]`; the field tier inherits PARALLAX's entire pre-registration including its funerals; and if the floor alone captures nearly everything the department finds — **that is not a failure of the product; that is the product**, and the funeral that prints is the resident's, not the floor's.
